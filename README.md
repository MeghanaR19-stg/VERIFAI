# VERIFAI — AI-Powered Bid Compliance Verification Platform

> Smart India Hackathon Prototype — AI-powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform for GeM Procurement

VERIFAI is a procurement compliance intelligence platform designed to help Procurement Officers evaluate bidder submissions faster and more consistently.

It converts tender requirements and bidder documents into an evidence-backed compliance assessment with:

- PASS / FAIL / REVIEW results
- Compliance scoring
- Risk assessment
- AI-assisted semantic analysis
- Government verification simulation
- Evidence Trace
- Procurement Officer decision workflow
- Complete audit trail

> **Important:** VERIFAI is a prototype. Government portal integrations shown in the application are simulated and do not represent live access to GSTN, Udyam, MCA, EPFO, ESIC, DigiLocker, or other government systems.

---

## Problem

Government procurement involves verification of multiple bidder requirements such as:

- Udyam / MSME registration
- GST status
- PAN / Income Tax information
- Make in India / local content
- EPFO / ESIC compliance
- Startup India
- NSIC registration
- OEM authorization
- DigiLocker documents
- Blacklisting / debarment
- Financial and experience requirements

The verification process can involve large numbers of documents, multiple information sources, manual comparisons, and repeated compliance checks.

This creates challenges around:

- Manual effort
- Document-heavy evaluation
- Cross-source verification
- Inconsistent interpretation
- Missing evidence
- Entity mismatches
- Auditability
- Evaluation time
